Selected technical projects

A focused selection of projects related to hydraulic modelling, transient analysis, state estimation and water-network simulation.

Observer-based state estimation

High-gain and sliding-mode observer workflows for estimating pressure heads, pipe flows and unknown hydraulic inputs.
